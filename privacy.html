BingoNingo Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026  ·  Operated by thecrazycat.com

This policy covers:

  • BingoNingo Caller (Android, com.jcat.bingoningo.app): run a bingo game, with player cards included;
  • BingoNingo Cards (Android, com.jcat.bingoningo.app.free): player cards only;
  • the web game at bingoningo.thecrazycat.com;
  • the TV screen at bingotv.thecrazycat.com.

Together these are called "BingoNingo" below.

In short: there are no accounts and no sign-in. Your cards, games and settings stay on your device. Ads, anonymous usage statistics and purchases are handled by the third-party services listed below. If you use the Smart TV feature, the game passes through our server on its way to your TV but is not stored. We do not sell your data.

1. Information stored on your device

BingoNingo keeps the following in the app's own storage on your phone, tablet or browser. It is not sent to us:

  • your bingo cards, share codes and card selections;
  • player codes and the initials you type for players;
  • game progress, the last game's winners and your settings (sound, speech, themes, TV screen options, venue name and prize text);
  • for the Smart TV feature, a session ID and access token that let the phone and TV reconnect.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data or your browser's site data, removes all of it.

2. Camera

The camera is used only when you choose to scan a QR code, for example a player's code. Scanning happens on your device. No pictures or video are stored or sent anywhere.

3. TV screen

The Caller app can show the game on a TV.

  • HDMI / USB-C, Big Screen mode and the TV window (web): the board is drawn on your own device. Nothing is sent to us. Screen mirroring or casting is handled by your phone and TV.
  • Smart TV: you open bingotv.thecrazycat.com in the TV's web browser and enter the code it shows into the app. While they are paired, your phone sends the current game to the TV through our relay server, which runs on Cloudflare. The connection is encrypted. The game includes:
    • called numbers and call words;
    • wins, with card numbers and the player initials you entered;
    • the near-win line;
    • the venue name and prize text you typed;
    • your theme colours and TV screen options.

    The relay passes this on to the paired TV and does not store it.

    To pair the devices and keep the service working, the relay briefly keeps:

    • the pairing code, which changes every 10 minutes;
    • a random session ID and access tokens. A session ends when you disconnect, or after 12 hours at most;
    • a short TV name taken from the TV browser's type (e.g. "Samsung TV"), shown in the app;
    • to stop people guessing codes, the IP address of a device that enters wrong codes. It is kept for a few minutes at most.

Cloudflare processes connection data such as IP addresses to deliver this service. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy.

The QR code on the TV screen opens thecrazycat.com/getbingoningo, a page with links to the apps.

4. Advertising

The free versions of BingoNingo show ads: Google AdMob in the Android apps, Google AdSense on the web game. Google may collect and use information about your device to show ads, including advertising ID, IP address and approximate location. This is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the UK, the EEA and other places where it is required, you are asked for your choices before personalised ads are shown. In the apps you can change them at any time from Ad preferences on the main menu. The Caller app offers a one-time Remove Ads purchase.

5. Usage statistics

The Android apps use Google Analytics for Firebase to count how the app is used. This includes:

  • games started and finished, and how long they last;
  • how many numbers were called;
  • which features are switched on;
  • the app version and device type.

It does not include player initials, card contents, venue or prize text. In the UK, the EEA and other places where consent is required, statistics are only collected if you agree to "store and/or access information on a device" in the consent form. You can change this at any time in Ad preferences. This helps us improve BingoNingo. See Firebase privacy.

6. Purchases

Remove Ads is bought through Google Play. Google handles the payment, and we never see your card details. Purchases are checked and restored using RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt from Google Play. See RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

7. Speech

Numbers and call words are spoken using your device's own text-to-speech engine. BingoNingo does not record audio.

8. Third-party services

  • Google AdMob / AdSense: ads. Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: usage statistics. Firebase privacy
  • Google Play Billing and RevenueCat: the Remove Ads purchase. RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare: hosts the web game, the TV screen and the Smart TV relay. Cloudflare Privacy Policy

9. Children

BingoNingo is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your choices and data deletion

  • Change your ad choices from Ad preferences on the main menu.
  • Disconnect a Smart TV at any time in ⚙ → 📺 TV screen → Smart TV.
  • Delete everything stored on your device by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it.
  • Ads, statistics and purchases data is held by the services above under their own policies. For anything else, email us and we will respond within 30 days.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always at https://thecrazycat.com/BingoNingo/privacy. Continued use of BingoNingo after changes means you accept the updated policy.
