Behind the apps — notes from the workshop.
QuizBlitz · Oct 2026
Six hours, then Android pulls the plug

Android 15 stops a foreground service after six hours, and a phone hosting a quiz night is exactly that. QuizBlitz now watches the clock: it stops cleanly between games, and if the cut-off lands mid-game it saves a snapshot so the game picks up where it left off.

QuizBlitz · Oct 2026
The backup that forgot every date

Question backups looked fine but quietly turned every date into a pair of numbers, so a restore wiped the cooldowns that stop the same question coming up twice. The fix was one line: read the raw value before it gets converted.

QuizBlitz · Oct 2026
Every question checked twice

Before launch the whole question bank went through review in batches: lint checks for jumbled numbers and answers that give themselves away, an AI first pass on the facts, a stronger model for the ones it wasn't sure about, and a human sign-off on every change.

My Messages · Sep 2026
The feature that mattered most came last

Everything else — inbox, digest, badge, widget — was in service of one thing: catching a message that actually needs someone's attention. Instant keyword alerts to a caregiver's email, and a check-in that notices when a phone's gone quiet, shipped in the final weeks before launch.

My Messages · Aug 2026
Five rounds to get a widget right

The home screen widget used to snap to three fixed sizes, so anything in between wasted space or got cramped. Rebuilt it to read its actual size continuously and lay itself out for whatever shape it's given — took five rounds against real on-device screenshots to get the spacing honest.

My Messages · Jul 2026
The gradient that broke the launcher, not the app

A bitmap-based gradient on the floating badge crashed — but not in our logs. It was crashing the phone's launcher trying to inflate a stale resource reference, which only showed up in the launcher's own logcat. Swapped it for layered flat colours instead.

My Messages · Jul 2026
Never punish a lapsed subscriber

Pro features now gate at render time only — your chosen colour theme or badge style is never overwritten if a subscription lapses. Resubscribe and everything you picked just comes back, automatically.

The studio

One maker, one cat, lots of ideas.

TheCrazyCat is a small independent developer in the UK, building Android apps that are playful to use and genuinely helpful. The goal is simple: apps that do one thing brilliantly, look good doing it, and respect your time.

BingoNingo was the first family — a full bingo system for clubs, pubs, charities and front-room games. RingReminder, My Messages and QuizBlitz followed it out of the workshop.
